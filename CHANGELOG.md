# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-30

Initial experimental catalogue profile: sources, activities, immutable
occurrences, evidence-backed assets, annotations, metadata and extensions.
Includes normative specification, Draft 2020-12 schemas, two synthetic archives,
offline reference validator, negative/positive tests, and CI configuration.

No released predecessor or automated migration exists. Self-contained content
packages, database adapters, scanning, merging and synchronization are future work.
